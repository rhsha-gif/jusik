# Investment evidence

Load the project’s investment and decision-record rules. Gather dated primary evidence for the thesis and counter-thesis; record uncertainty, blind spots and checkable invalidation conditions. Preserve immutable decision history and required review of consequential judgments. Evidence gathering and analysis do not authorize trades, broker writes or automated decisions.

A small evidence lookup can stay with the current agent. Separate research, analysis and independent review when the scope or stakes require it; `examples/plan-invest-evidence.json` is an optional larger-work example. Preserve required vault access and route to the provider that actually supports it before execution.
